02 · RULEBOOK

Important
instructions.

01 · ABOUT URJAOpen-ended Energy Innovation Working Model Competition.

Focus on renewable, sustainable and efficient use of energy. Explore generation, conservation, efficiency, conversion, storage, recovery, utilization or smart management.

02 · SCOPE OF INNOVATIONNo fixed problem statement or technology.

Ideas may cover renewable energy, energy conservation, efficiency, storage, smart systems, energy recovery, and sustainable solutions for homes, communities, agriculture or remote areas.

03 · TEAM FORMATIONMaximum 4 participants per team.

Nominate one primary presenter. The registered team must develop and present the submitted model.

04 · WORKING MODELA working model is mandatory.

The model must demonstrate its intended working principle, address a clear energy-related problem/opportunity, be safe to operate, and be transported, assembled and operated by the team.

05 · PRESENTATION & DEMOMaximum 10 minutes per team.

Explain the problem, solution, working principle, innovation and application. Demonstrate the model within the allotted time, followed by jury questions.

06 · ORIGINALITYThe core idea and implementation should be the team's own work.

External references, existing technologies and standard components may be used, but copied work must not be presented as original. Significant external technology should be acknowledged.

07 · EVALUATIONModels will be evaluated on key technical and practical aspects.

Working Principle • Innovation • Functionality • Practicality & Feasibility • Sustainability / Energy Impact • Presentation & Q&A.

08 · REGISTRATION & PARTICIPATION₹100 per participant.

Bring the registered working model on 30 Sep 2026. Participation certificates will be provided; prizes worth ₹8,000 will be awarded as per organizers' decision.
