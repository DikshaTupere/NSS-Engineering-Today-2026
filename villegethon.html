SCITECH VILLEGETHON
Socio-Tech Hackathon

What is SCITECH VILLEGETHON?

SCITECH VILLEGETHON is a socio-tech hackathon where participants identify real problems faced by communities, villages and society, and turn engineering ideas into practical, feasible solutions that create meaningful impact.

It encourages teams to look beyond conventional problem statements, understand real-world needs and build solutions that are useful, affordable, scalable and relevant to the community.

03 · RULEBOOK

Important
instructions.

01 · THEMEDevelop innovative, practical and sustainable technology-based solutions for real-world village and rural challenges.

Solutions should demonstrate meaningful social, economic or environmental impact in rural communities.

02 · PROJECT REQUIREMENTSSubmit a PPT explaining the problem, solution, technical approach and expected impact.

Prototype, working model or demonstration model should be developed wherever applicable, with an implementation approach and estimated cost plan.

03 · TEAM FORMATIONMaximum 4 participants per team.

Each participant can be part of only one team. Nominate one primary presenter; all members should understand the project and participate in the presentation/demo.

04 · SUBMISSION & SCHEDULE27 Sep — Final PPT / Project Submission

28 Sep — Screening • 29 Sep — Camera-Ready Submission • 30 Sep — SCITECH VILLEGETHON Event.

05 · PRESENTATION & DEMOMaximum 10 minutes per team.

5 min PPT • 3 min Prototype/Working Model/Demo • 2 min Jury Q&A. Be ready to explain feasibility, cost, implementation, limitations, scalability and impact.

06 · ORIGINALITY & SAFETYProjects must be original and technically meaningful.

Plagiarism or presenting another team's work may lead to disqualification. Unsafe demonstrations may be stopped by organizers.

07 · MODE OF PARTICIPATIONHybrid Mode.

Out-of-state participants may participate online as specified. Online teams must ensure stable internet, camera/audio and a clear demonstration setup.

08 · REGISTRATION₹100 per participant.

Registration must be completed through the official process. Participation certificates will be provided to all participants.
